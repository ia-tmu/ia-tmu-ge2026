The objective is to develop a visualization method that allows viewers to intuitively and quantitatively understand players’ decision-making contexts and characteristics. While detailed data, such as shooting percentages and locations, are increasingly accessible, they are often presented separately from the video, hindering a seamless viewing and analytical experience.

Specifically, the system features shot success rates displayed above players' heads, detailed information panels, and heatmaps synchronized with player positions.
