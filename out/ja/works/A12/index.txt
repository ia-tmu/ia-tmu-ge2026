1d:T59c,This sole was developed with the concept of “encouraging slower walking.” Recent years have seen movements and research recognizing the value of walking slowly. A prime example is the health practice known as “meditative walking.” These developments indicate a certain demand for slower walking. However, walking speeds in urban areas have increased over the past few decades. Modern people, constantly pressed for time and rushing to complete tasks, have largely stopped walking slowly.
