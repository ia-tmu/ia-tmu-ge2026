1d:T735,Birds possess a wide range of appealing qualities, including the patterns of their feathers, the forms of their bodies, and their rich coloration. This study focuses on the beauty and rhythmic forms unique to each species as found in these details, and aims to demonstrate new graphic possibilities of birds by extracting and reconstructing these elements as patterns.
